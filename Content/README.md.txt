# 📚 Training Content

## What Is Phishing?
Phishing is a cyberattack where attackers impersonate trusted organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, or login credentials.

## Why Phishing Is Dangerous
- Account compromise
- Financial loss
- Identity theft
- Entry point for ransomware and data breaches

## Common Types of Phishing
- Email Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Whaling
- Smishing (SMS)
- Vishing (Voice)

## Recognizing Phishing Emails
- Suspicious sender addresses
- Generic greetings
- Urgent or threatening language
- Unexpected links or attachments
- Poor grammar or spelling

## Identifying Fake Websites
- Misspelled domain names
- Missing HTTPS
- Poor website design
- Requests for unnecessary information

## Social Engineering Tactics
- Urgency
- Fear
- Authority
- Trust
- Curiosity

## Best Practices
- Verify sender identity
- Do not click suspicious links
- Never share passwords
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Report suspicious messages immediately
